About

A bit About ME!

Hei Everyone, name is Muhammad Fadhilah, or call me Fadhil or maybe Ali. I'm a Mobile video editor and poster designer based in Surabaya, 12 grade at SMK Darma Siswa 1 Sidoarjo.

On the editing side I use Alight Motion, Canva, and Capcut. Good at color grading and clean transitions that keep viewers watching. On the design side I build poster layouts with strong typography.

I was an Internship at Polygron Group Insera Sena at HRGA Department for 6 Month. And currently at PT. E-T-A Indonesia as IT Intern at IT Department. Talking about experience, i also the one who designed my Class Logo, Instagram Feeds Theme&Design, and all the events going.
